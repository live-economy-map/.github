# 📖 Overview

NEXA – Citizenship Service is an offline-first digital government platform that enables citizens to seamlessly access essential public services through a secure and intelligent system.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between citizens and government institutions by simplifying service delivery, reducing unnecessary physical visits, and ensuring accessibility regardless of internet availability.

---

# ❗️ Problem Statement

Accessing essential public services remains a challenge for many citizens due to limited digital accessibility, fragmented service systems, long distances to government offices, and unreliable internet connectivity in many areas.

Millions of people face difficulties when applying for documents, reporting issues, accessing information, and receiving government services because existing systems often require physical visits, stable internet connections, and complex procedures.

This creates delays, increases administrative burden, and leaves communities in remote areas with limited access to important services.
